# Privacy policy

_BetterSEQTA+ Lite, last updated 2 November 2026_

BetterSEQTA+ Lite is a browser extension that restyles and improves the SEQTA Learn and SEQTA Engage school portals. It works entirely in your browser.

## What it reads

On SEQTA pages only, the extension reads the page and your school's SEQTA data (such as your timetable, notices, messages and assessments) so it can display them differently. It does this using your existing SEQTA login, inside your browser.

## What it stores

Your settings (theme, colours, layout and similar preferences) are saved in your browser's extension storage on your device. They are deleted when you remove the extension.

## What it sends

The Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons versions do **not** collect, sell or share any personal data, and do not send your SEQTA data anywhere. They only download public content:

- the list of themes from the theme store (betterseqta.org), when you open the store
- news headlines (newsapi.org), when you open the news page
- fonts (Google Fonts)

These requests contain no information about you beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address).

### GitHub builds only: Google Calendar sync

The builds published on GitHub (not the store versions) include an optional Google Calendar sync. Only if you connect it:

- your timetable (lesson names, times, rooms and teachers) is written to a calendar the extension creates in **your** Google account
- Google sign-in goes through accounts.betterseqta.org, which exchanges and refreshes the Google access tokens

You can disconnect it at any time in Settings.
